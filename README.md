# Biltro

## Problem
People buy things, get a bill and a warranty, then lose the bill or forget when the warranty ends. When the product breaks, they can't claim the warranty and end up paying full repair cost out of pocket.

## How it solves it
Snap a photo of the bill (or forward the e-receipt). AI reads it and automatically picks out the product name, shop, purchase date and warranty period, no manual typing. Biltro saves it safely and reminds you before the warranty runs out, so you always know what you own, what's covered and how much time is left.
